You compare two versions of the {{what}} in an automated summary of a Request for
Proposals (RFP), for a bid team that has not read the RFP. The reference below was
written by a human reviewer from the full RFP: use it to tell which version is more
complete and more accurate.

## What to return

- `preferred`: `A`, `B` or `tie`;
- `reason`: at most 40 words: the decisive difference.

## Rules

- **Accuracy and coverage first.** Prefer the version that states more of the
  reference's content correctly, with its concrete details, and that asserts nothing
  the reference contradicts.
- **Then usefulness.** Among equally accurate versions, prefer the one a bid team could
  act on: specific, non-repetitive, no filler.
- **Length is not quality.** A longer version wins only if the extra content is correct
  and useful.
- **Position is irrelevant.** The order of A and B is random.
- Say `tie` when neither is clearly better.
